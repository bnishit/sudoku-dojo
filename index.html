Hints that teach

A coach, not a cheat sheet.

Most apps just fill in a cell. Here a hint comes in three levels, so you learn something every time:

  • Level 1: names the technique. "Try a Naked Pair."
  • Level 2: lights up where to look.
  • Level 3: shows the full move and why it works, then applies it for you.

It also catches your mistakes first: a wrong digit, or a note that rubbed out the right answer.

Get faster

Train your eye, not just your brain.

⚡ Technique Rush🎯 Practice any technique🧩 Mini 6×6🔒 Quick fill

  • Rush: 90 seconds to spot as many techniques as you can. Each find adds 3 seconds.
  • Practice: 30 real positions for every technique. Keep going until it's automatic.
  • Mini 6×6: a full game in a few minutes. A great warm-up.
  • Quick fill: pick a digit once, then tap cell after cell.
