Sudoku Belts

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Sudoku Belts collects no data about you. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. We run no app servers and do not receive your progress.

The website is a set of static files hosted by Vercel, which may keep standard access logs (such as IP address and time) for security. The iPhone app makes no network requests.

Your progress stays with you

On the web, progress and settings stay in your browser’s storage. On iPhone, they stay on your phone, inside the app. They do not sync across devices on their own.

Clearing browser data or deleting the iPhone app can erase your progress. Keep a backup if you want to restore it later.

Backups are your choice

A backup file is created only when you tap Save backup file under Me → Back up your progress. On iPhone, choose where to save or share it. On the web, your browser saves the download in its chosen location. We do not receive a copy.

Reminders stay on your phone

The optional daily reminder on iPhone is a local notification: your phone schedules and shows it. There is no push server. You can turn it off under Me → Daily reminder.

On the web, you can choose to add a reminder to your own calendar. Your calendar handles that reminder.

Play offline

The iPhone app works offline from the first launch. On the web, load and install the app first so it is ready for offline play. No internet connection is needed to solve puzzles or save progress.

Questions?

Open an issue on GitHub. GitHub is a separate service. Anything you post in a public issue can be read by others, so leave out private details and backup files.
