Sudoku Belts

Support

Need help with Sudoku Belts? Open an issue on GitHub. Tell us whether you use the web or iPhone app, what you tried, and what happened. A screenshot can help.

GitHub issues are public. Leave out private details and backup files.

Where is my progress?

Open Me to see your progress. It is saved only on this device: in browser storage on the web, or inside the app on iPhone. It does not sync across devices. Clearing browser data or deleting the iPhone app can erase it. We have no server copy to recover.

How do I back up or restore?

Go to Me → Back up your progress → Save backup file. On iPhone, choose where to save it, such as Files. On the web, keep the file from your browser’s downloads somewhere safe.

To restore, open Me → Back up your progress → Restore from file, choose your saved backup, and confirm. Restoring replaces the progress on this device. Save a fresh backup first if you want to keep the current progress.

How do I turn reminders off?

On iPhone, open Me → Daily reminder and turn the switch off. You can also turn off Sudoku Belts notifications in your iPhone’s Settings.

If you added a calendar reminder from the web app, delete the repeating Sudoku Belts event in your calendar. The web app cannot remove it for you.

Is it free?

Yes. Version 1 is fully free: all lessons, puzzles, and practice modes. No ads, purchases, or account needed.
