## Description

Learn how to find your next Sudoku move.

Sudoku Belts is for players who can solve easy puzzles but get stuck as they get harder. Short lessons explain a pattern, show it on a real board, and let you try it yourself.

LEARN, THEN TRY
Follow a path from White Belt onward. Learn to scan a board, spot a hidden single, and work with notes: small digits that show what could still fit in a cell. Practise one skill or mix several without being told which one to use.

HELP THAT TELLS YOU WHY
Hints explain a move on the board in front of you. Ask for a small nudge or more help. Hints never cost stars. The app shows guided and solo solves separately.

A COACH FROM YOUR OWN PLAY
Once you have played enough, Your blind spot shows a kind of move you tend to miss. Revisit saved moments where you got stuck and try them again. The coach needs enough moves before it can show a pattern.

A LITTLE PRACTICE EACH DAY
Play the daily puzzle, choose from five difficulty levels, or start with a smaller 6 by 6 board. Set a weekly goal, see your practice days, and choose an optional daily reminder.

FREE, QUIET, AND OFFLINE
All v1 lessons and puzzles are free. No ads, no purchases, no account, and no tracking. Play offline from the first launch. Your progress stays on your iPhone. You can save a backup file and restore it later; progress does not sync across devices on its own.

Take your time. Try a hint. Find the next move.
